Linking more channels

Several Google accounts, several channels each, one OAuth client

One client JSON covers every account and channel

You upload the client_secret.json once. Every channel you link afterwards is a separate Google consent that produces its own refresh token, stored encrypted against that channel. You never need a second client JSON.

Do not create extra Google Cloud projects or OAuth clients to get more quota. That goes against YouTube's API terms. The quota belongs to the one project behind your client JSON and is shared by all channels.

Each channel is linked separately

Google gives this app no way to list or add all of an account's Brand channels in one go. Each authorization is tied to the single channel you pick in Google's chooser, and the YouTube API has no call that lists the others for a normal account. So a Google account with three channels needs three links, one per channel. The Channels page groups the results under the Google account they belong to, like YouTube's account switcher.

Which Google account can link which channel

Google can only offer the channels that belong to the account you sign in with. The Brand-channel chooser lists the channels that account owns or manages as a Brand Account. If the chooser never appears, or you come back with No new channel was added, the account you signed in with does not own the channel you wanted.

Channels you were only invited to manage in YouTube Studio do not count. YouTube's own help states that people invited through channel permissions "can't manage via YouTube APIs" and that invited users can't access "YouTube APIs", even though the owner can (YouTube Help: channel permissions, migrating from Brand Account access). So this app cannot be authorized for such a channel through a Google account that was merely invited. This is a limit of Google's API, not something the panel can work around.

The supported way is therefore: sign in with the Google account that owns the channel (the Owner shown in YouTube Studio → Settings → Permissions, or the Brand Account's owner or manager in that account's Google settings), and link it from there. Link each channel under the Gmail that owns it; you do not need every Gmail to have access to every channel. Do not add extra Google Cloud projects or OAuth clients to get around this: it does not help, and it breaks YouTube's terms.

Before you add a Google account: add it as a test user

While your OAuth consent screen is in Testing, only listed test users can sign in. For every Google account you want to link, including accounts that only manage Brand channels:

  1. Open Google Cloud Console → APIs & Services → OAuth consent screen (shown as Audience in the newer console) for the project that owns your client JSON.
  2. Under Test users, choose Add users and enter that account's Gmail address.
  3. Save. Google allows up to 100 test users.

If you skip this, Google shows Access blocked: … has not completed the Google verification process and the link fails. Also note that Google expires refresh tokens after 7 days for projects left in Testing status; channels then show needs re-auth until you re-authorize them. Check your consent screen's publishing status in the Cloud Console if channels keep dropping.

Link each channel

  1. Make sure each channel is a Brand Account channel and that the Google account you sign in with is an Owner or Manager of it (YouTube Studio → Settings → Permissions).
  2. First channel of a new Google account: open Channels → Link a channel from another Google account. Another channel of an account you already linked: use that account's Add another channel from this account button. It pre-selects the account so Google jumps straight to that account's channel chooser.
  3. The panel asks Google to show its account chooser. Choose the Google account.
  4. If that account manages Brand Accounts, Google then asks which channel to grant access to. Pick the one you want to add now.
  5. Google hasn't verified this app: because this is your own private app you will see this warning. Choose Advanced, then Go to … (unsafe). On the permissions screen leave every box ticked (the email one files the channel under its account, and the YouTube management one is what allows deleting videos) and press Continue. If a required permission is unticked the link is refused and you are asked to try again.
  6. The panel answers either Linked new channel: … or No new channel was added: Google returned … which was already linked, and names the Google account. The second answer means Google offered no other channel for the account you signed in with (see "Which Google account can link which channel" above).
  7. Repeat for every other channel, choosing a different one in the chooser each time.

New channels appear in the Telegram bot immediately; nothing needs a restart. The first channel you link becomes the default. You can change the default, and give each channel a display label (shown in the bot and panel), on the Channels page.

When more than one channel is active the bot asks where each video should go, with the channels grouped under their Google account and the default listed first. Channels that need re-authorization are left out of that list, and the bot says which ones.

Channels listed under "Account not recorded"

Channels linked before the account email was recorded, and any link where the email permission was unticked, are listed under Account not recorded — re-authorize to update. They keep working. Use Re-authorize on each one, pick that same channel in Google's chooser, and leave all permissions ticked; it then moves under its Google account.

If Google skips the chooser

The panel asks Google to show the chooser every time, but Google decides what to display. If you land back in the panel with was already linked, or the wrong channel got linked:

  • Start the link again in a private/incognito window and sign in with only the Google account that manages the channels.
  • Remove this app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, then link again so Google asks afresh.
  • Check you are an Owner or Manager of the channel you want and that it is a Brand Account channel.
  • To fix a channel that shows needs re-auth, use its Re-authorize button and pick that same channel in the chooser. Picking a different one links that one instead, and the original stays broken.

Uploads longer than 15 minutes

YouTube only accepts videos over 15 minutes from a channel whose phone number is verified. Verify each channel separately at youtube.com/verify. Until then a long upload to that channel fails even though the others work.

Quota is one shared pool

Quota is counted per Google Cloud project, not per channel or per Google account. Every upload to any channel draws from the same daily pool, and the daily upload allowance is shared too (see the Dashboard). The per-channel upload counts on the Channels page are statistics only; they do not give each channel its own quota. Quota resets at midnight Pacific time.
