Linking more channels

Several YouTube channels, one Google account, one OAuth client

One client JSON covers every channel

You upload the client_secret.json once. Every channel you link afterwards is a separate Google consent that produces its own refresh token, stored encrypted against that channel. You never need a second client JSON.

Do not create extra Google Cloud projects or OAuth clients to get more quota. That goes against YouTube's API terms. The quota belongs to the one project behind your client JSON and is shared by all channels.

Link each channel

  1. Make sure each channel is a Brand Account channel and that the Google account you sign in with is an Owner or Manager of it (YouTube Studio → Settings → Permissions).
  2. Open Channels → Link a channel and follow Google's consent screens.
  3. The panel asks Google to show its account chooser first. Choose your Google account.
  4. If that account manages Brand Accounts, Google then asks which channel to grant access to. Pick the one you want to add now.
  5. Approve the consent screen. The panel answers either Linked new channel: … or … was already linked.
  6. Repeat from step 2 for every other channel, choosing a different one each time.

New channels appear in the Telegram bot immediately; nothing needs a restart. The first channel you link becomes the default. You can change the default, and give each channel a display label (shown in the bot and panel), on the Channels page.

When more than one channel is active the bot asks where each video should go, with the default listed first. Channels that need re-authorization are left out of that list, and the bot says which ones.

If Google skips the chooser

The panel asks Google to show the chooser every time, but Google decides what to display. If you land back in the panel with was already linked, or the wrong channel got linked:

  • Start the link again in a private/incognito window and sign in with only the Google account that manages the channels.
  • Remove this app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, then link again so Google asks afresh.
  • Check you are an Owner or Manager of the channel you want and that it is a Brand Account channel.
  • To fix a channel that shows needs re-auth, use its Re-authorize button and pick that same channel in the chooser. Picking a different one links that one instead, and the original stays broken.

Uploads longer than 15 minutes

YouTube only accepts videos over 15 minutes from a channel whose phone number is verified. Verify each channel separately at youtube.com/verify. Until then a long upload to that channel fails even though the others work.

Quota is one shared pool

Quota is counted per Google Cloud project, not per channel. Every upload to any channel draws from the same daily pool, and the daily upload allowance is shared too (see the Dashboard). The per-channel upload counts on the Channels page are statistics only; they do not give each channel its own quota. Quota resets at midnight Pacific time.
